# Doodle Defense

A free test page for **Doodle Defense**, an original drawing game.

Play it here: https://chirowsan.github.io/doodle-defense/

The game fills the screen like a lined notebook page. Pick a crayon and a drawing tool, then draw a path with your finger. Friendly doodles hatch on the line and walk along it. Draw a loop and they circle as guards. Crossing colors makes a special spot: blue × red is a splash burst, green × blue is a bouncy bush, and red × green is a thorn patch. A triangle becomes a launch pad, and a loop around a star grabs it.

**Drawing tools** cost different amounts of ink: the **Pen** is cheap, thin, and precise but weaker; the **Brush** is thick, sturdy, and more expensive; the **Highlighter** covers a wide area and can slow or reveal monsters, but it gets chewed through quickly.

Each crayon is labeled so you can see what it does: **Blue** slows monsters, **Red** tickles them, **Green** is a wall, **Zap** shocks, **Blink** swaps ends, and the eraser rubs a line out. The first time you meet a color, tool, combo, monster, or hazard, a short pop-up explains it. Open **How to play** from the menu or the pause screen for the full list, including counters. Before every wave, a **scouting preview** names what's coming and how to beat it. Draw your plan, then press **Start wave**.

Green walls block monsters. If there's a gap they walk around; otherwise they chew through the wall (it cracks, then snaps). Armored monsters and bosses break walls faster. Hoppers jump over them.

Before each page you pick a few **seed packets** (3 on early pages, 4 later). Place them on the page: an **Inkwell** slowly fills during a wave and drops ink you tap to collect (drops fade if you leave them). Other packets unlock as you go: a **Decoy** that monsters rush to chew, a **Sharpener** turret, a **Glue pad**, a **Bumper**, and a **Reservoir** that holds more ink. After you place one during a wave, it recharges before you can place it again. Monsters chew your buildings too, so where you put them matters. Crayon lines help: blue waters an Inkwell, red sharpens a Sharpener, and green fences any part from chewing.

You can also **design your own defenders** from the menu or the loadout screen. Pick a color, face, size, a job (Poke, Slow, Mend, Gather, or Guard), and up to two traits, then spend ink to send them along a drawn line. They patrol back and forth. You can save three designs on your device.

Each page has a tight **ink budget** that barely refills, and you start with only part of it, so you can't draw a full defense right away. Place an **Inkwell** early and tap the drops to build up; collecting a star also refunds 20 ink. Drawing mid-wave costs extra, so every line is a choice. There are three worlds with five pages plus a boss in each, and an Endless Sketch mode. Mixed types show up from the early pages, so one color combo won't solve every wave. Some scribbles hop over vines, nibble lines, split in two, hide until they touch blue, fly over lines, wear armor, or heal others. Newer types include **Diggers** that tunnel under lines, **Ink-nibs** that steal drops, **Blotlings** that leave no-draw blots, **Cover-bugs** that shield others, **Zippers** that swarm, and **Drop-fakers** that pretend to be ink drops. Bosses change tactics as they get hurt. Beat bosses to unlock yellow Zap and purple Blink. Finish a page for up to three stars (clear it, let nothing reach the house, and keep 35% of your ink after using a combo or launch pad).

Normal is the default and is meant to take real planning. Easy is gentler, Hard is tougher, and Nightmare unlocks after you clear the last page on Hard. Battery saver defaults to Auto. Three leaks lose a page on Normal.

This is our own design. There are no soldiers, guns, or gore.
