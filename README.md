# Doodle Defense

A free test page for **Doodle Defense**, an original drawing game.

Play it here: https://chirowsan.github.io/doodle-defense/

Pick a crayon and draw a path with your finger. Friendly doodles hatch on the line and walk along it. Draw a loop and they circle as guards. Crossing colors makes a special spot: blue × red is a splash burst, green × blue is a bouncy bush, and red × green is a thorn patch. A triangle becomes a launch pad, and a loop around a star grabs it.

Each crayon is labeled so you can see what it does: **Blue** slows monsters, **Red** tickles them, **Green** is a wall, **Zap** shocks, **Blink** swaps ends, and the eraser rubs a line out. The first time you meet a color, combo, monster, or hazard, a short tip explains it. Open **How to play** from the menu or the pause screen for the full list, including counters. Before every wave, a **scouting preview** names what's coming and how to beat it. Draw your plan, then press **Start wave**.

Green walls block monsters. If there's a gap they walk around; otherwise they chew through the wall (it cracks, then snaps). Armored monsters and bosses break walls faster. Hoppers jump over them.

Each page has a tight **ink budget** that barely refills, and drawing mid-wave costs extra, so every line is a choice. There are three worlds with five pages plus a boss in each, and an Endless Sketch mode. Some scribbles hop over vines, nibble lines, split in two, hide until they touch blue, fly over lines, wear armor, or heal others. Bosses change tactics as they get hurt. Beat bosses to unlock yellow Zap and purple Blink. Finish a page for up to three stars (clear it, let nothing reach the house, and keep 35% of your ink after using a combo or launch pad).

Normal is the default and is meant to take real planning. Easy is gentler, Hard is tougher, and Nightmare unlocks after you clear the last page on Hard. Battery saver defaults to Auto. Three leaks lose a page on Normal.

This is our own design. There are no soldiers, guns, or gore.
