# Doodle Defense

A free test page for **Doodle Defense**, an original kids' drawing game (ages 5–10).

Play it here: https://chirowsan.github.io/doodle-defense/

Pick a crayon and draw a path with your finger. Friendly doodles hatch on the line and walk along it. Draw a loop and they circle as guards. Crossing colors makes a special spot: blue × red is a splash burst, green × blue is a bouncy bush, and red × green is a thorn patch. A triangle becomes a launch pad, and a loop around a star grabs it.

There are three worlds with five pages plus a boss in each, and an Endless Sketch mode. Some scribbles hop over vines, nibble lines, split in two, or hide until they touch blue. Beat bosses to unlock yellow Zap and purple Blink. Finish a page for up to three stars.

This is our own design. There are no fixed lanes or towers, and no soldiers, guns, or gore. Normal is the default (Easy is there for little kids, and Hard is a tougher challenge). Battery saver defaults to Auto.
