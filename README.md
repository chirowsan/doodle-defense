# Doodle Defense

A free test page for **Doodle Defense**, an original kids' drawing game (ages 5–10).

Play it here: https://chirowsan.github.io/doodle-defense/

Pick a crayon and draw a path with your finger. Friendly doodles hatch on the line and walk along it. Draw a loop and they circle as guards. Crossing colors makes a special spot: blue × red is a splash burst, green × blue is a bouncy bush, and red × green is a thorn patch. A triangle becomes a launch pad, and a loop around a star grabs it.

Before every wave you get a **scouting preview** of what is coming and where it will walk. Draw your plan, then press **Start wave**. Each page has a tight **ink budget** that barely refills, and drawing mid-wave costs extra, so every line is a choice.

There are three worlds with five pages plus a boss in each, and an Endless Sketch mode. Some scribbles hop over vines, nibble lines, split in two, hide until they touch blue, fly over lines, wear armor, or heal others. Bosses change tactics as they get hurt. Beat bosses to unlock yellow Zap and purple Blink. Finish a page for up to three stars (clear it, let nothing reach the house, and keep 35% of your ink after using a combo or launch pad).

Normal is the default and is meant to take real planning. Easy is there for little kids, Hard is tougher, and Nightmare unlocks after you clear the last page on Hard. Battery saver defaults to Auto. Three leaks lose a page on Normal.

This is our own design. There are no soldiers, guns, or gore.
