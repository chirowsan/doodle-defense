# Doodle Defense

A free test page for **Doodle Defense**, an original kids' drawing game (ages 5–10).

Play it here: https://chirowsan.github.io/doodle-defense/

Pick a crayon and draw a path with your finger. Friendly doodles hatch on the line and walk along it. Draw a loop and they circle as guards. Blue lines slow scribble monsters, red lines are speedy, and green lines grow vine walls. Ink runs out as you draw and fills back up. On some pages you protect the sticker house; on others you collect stars.

This is our own design. There are no fixed lanes or towers, and no soldiers, guns, or gore. Easy is the default, and battery saver defaults to Auto.
