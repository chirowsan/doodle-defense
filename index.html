How to play

You draw the paths. Before each wave, read the scouting preview, draw your plan, then press Start wave. Ink is a tight budget and barely refills by itself, so plant Inkwells and tap the ink drops they make. Drawing mid-wave costs extra. On Normal, three leaks lose the page.

Colors

  • Blue: slow. A cheap line that slows monsters. Also reveals sneaky ones.
  • Red: tickle. Doodles hatch, run the line, and poke monsters. Costs more ink, but they move fast.
  • Green: wall. Blocks monsters. They will chew through it, and armored or boss ones break it faster. Hoppers jump over walls.
  • Zap: shock. Zaps nearby ground monsters, then fizzles after a few hits. Unlock it by beating the first boss.
  • Blink: swap. Anyone who touches an end swaps to the other end. Unlock it by beating the second boss.
  • Eraser. Rub out a line and get a little ink back.

Combos and shapes

  • Blue × red = splash burst. Hurts everything nearby, even flyers and sneaky ones.
  • Red × green = thorns. Hurts ground monsters standing in it. Best against armored ones.
  • Green × blue = bouncy bush. A springy patch that helps your doodles.
  • Triangle = launch pad. Doodles on it fly at nearby monsters. This is how you hit flyers (splashes work too).
  • A loop around a star grabs it. Loops also make doodles walk in a circle as guards.

Drawing tools

  • Pen (cheap): a thin, precise line. Breaks easily.
  • Brush (costly): a thick, sturdy stroke with a wider effect.
  • Highlighter (medium): a wide translucent tint. Slows and reveals in its width, but monsters chew it fast.

The tool applies on top of the crayon color (blue still slows, green still walls, and so on).

Custom doodles

From the menu you can design a buddy: pick a color, face, size, a role (Poke, Slow, Mend, Gather, or Guard) and a trait or two. Then tap a drawn line to send them out. They patrol back and forth. Bigger or stronger choices cost more ink.

Components (seed packets)

Before each page, pick a few packets. Tap a packet, then tap the page to place it. It costs ink, then recharges during a wave. Components fill, shoot or help on their own; ink drops fade, so tap them quickly. Red shading shows where monsters will chew.

    Any component touching a green line is fenced: it takes 40% less chewing. Monsters chew components they walk near, just like walls.

    Monsters and counters

    • Scribble: basic walker. Your doodles poke it.
    • Hopper: jumps green walls. Use tickles, blue, or thorns.
    • Eraser-bug: eats your lines. Give it a short decoy to chew.
    • Splitter: becomes two when hurt. Keep hitting both.
    • Sneaky: stays hidden until it touches blue, a puddle, or a splash.
    • Armored: tickles barely work. Use thorns, Zap, a splash, or a launch pad.
    • Healer: mends others. Pop it first with a splash or thorns.
    • Flyer: soars over lines and walls. Use a triangle launch pad or a splash.
    • Digger: burrows under lines. Hit it with a splash, or poke it when it pops up.
    • Ink-nib: steals ink drops. Collect them first, or poke it.
    • Blotling: leaves a no-draw blot when popped. Take it out early and away from your lines.
    • Cover-bug: shields nearby monsters. Pop the Cover-bug first.
    • Zipper: a tiny fast swarm. Slow them with blue, glue, or a splash.
    • Drop-faker: disguises as an ink drop. Don't tap it — poke it.
    • Bosses (Big Smudge, Nibblewick, Hidey Storm) change tactics as they get hurt.

    Page hazards

    • Puddle: a line through it makes swimmers, and monsters slow down in the water.
    • Coffee stain: drawing here costs double ink.
    • Ink blot: a no-draw zone. Your line stops if it hits one.
    • Fold: the folded part of the page cuts your line.
    • Wind: pushes your line as you draw.

    Stars: clear the page, let nothing reach the house, and keep 35% of your ink after using a combo or launch pad. Clear Hard to unlock Nightmare.
