How to play

There are no ready-made lanes. You draw the paths with your finger, and friendly doodles walk along your line.

Blue makes a watery trail that slows scribbles. Red is speedy. Green grows a vine wall that blocks the way.

Draw a loop and doodles walk in a circle to guard. Draw through a puddle to make swimmers. The eraser rubs out a line.

Ink is used up as you draw, then fills back in. Protect the sticker house, or collect stars — each page tells you which.
