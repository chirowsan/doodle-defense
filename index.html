How to play

You draw the paths. Before each wave you get a scouting preview: the dotted trails show where scribbles walk and what is coming. Draw, then press Start wave.

Your ink budget barely refills, so every line is a choice. Drawing during a wave costs extra. Red is fast but pricey, blue is cheap and slows, green vines wear down, yellow Zap fizzles out, purple Blink swaps the two ends.

Combos: blue × red = splash, green × blue = bush, red × green = thorns. A triangle is a launch pad. A loop around a star grabs it.

Armored ones shrug off tickles, flyers soar over lines, healers mend others, eraser-bugs eat lines, hoppers jump vines, sneaky ones hide until blue. Bosses change tactics.

Only 3 leaks and the page is smudged. Stars: clear it, keep 25% of your ink, and finish with no leaks and a combo. Clear Hard to unlock Nightmare.
